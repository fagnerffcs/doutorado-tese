\section{Design Science Research}
\label{sec:dsr-approach}

Unlike traditional natural sciences that seek to understand reality (positivism) or social sciences that seek to interpret it (interpretivism), Design Science is rooted in pragmatic epistemology. Its fundamental goal is to transform operational reality through the creation and rigorous evaluation of innovative artifacts.

As defined by \citeonline{simon_1969} and consolidated by \citeonline{hevner_2004}, DSR involves the iterative process of designing artifacts to solve observed problems, making prescriptive research contributions, and rigorously evaluating the resulting designs. In the context of this thesis, the primary artifact is \textbf{PANOPTES}, a reference architecture and multimodal correlation engine for cloud-native observability.

The artifacts produced in this research span three complementary levels of abstraction:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Architecture (Model):} A structural reference framework defining the Three Pillars of observability (Metrics, Logs, Traces), their semantic bindings, and closed-loop feedback pathways \cite{jansen_2005, capilla_2016}.
    \item \textbf{Method (Algorithmic RCA \& MADR Protocol):} A deterministic, multimodal Root Cause Analysis (RCA) method utilizing exclusive span self-time decomposition ($T_{\text{self}} = T_{\text{span}} - \sum T_{\text{children}}$) and L4/L7 peer attribution \cite{sigelman_2010, wang_2024, barata_2026}, governed by Markdown Architectural Decision Records (MADR) \cite{nygard_2011, kopp_2018}.
    \item \textbf{Instantiation:} A concrete Infrastructure as Code (IaC) implementation (K3d/Multipass cluster provisioning scripts, Ansible playbooks, Helm charts, OpenTelemetry Collector pipelines, and Python orchestration harnesses) that operationalizes the architecture in a reproducible Kubernetes environment.
\end{itemize}
