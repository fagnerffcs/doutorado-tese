\chapter{Results and Discussion}
\label{chap:results-and-discussion}

This chapter presents the empirical findings obtained from the $2 \times 2$ factorial experimental design. The evaluation is structured across two core dimensions: steady-state resource overhead (Scenarios~1 and 2) and fault-diagnostic performance under injected degradation (Scenarios~3 and the dual-phase Scenario 4a and 4b). 

To ensure strict scientific replicability, eliminate human operator bias, and mitigate environmental desynchronization (such as clock drift), the entire data collection process was governed by deterministic out-of-band orchestration scripts implemented in Python.
