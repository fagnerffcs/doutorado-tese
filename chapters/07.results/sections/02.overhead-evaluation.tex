\section{Steady-State Resource Overhead Evaluation (Scenarios 1 and 2)}
\label{sec:steady-state-overhead-results}

To evaluate the steady-state computational and memory footprint imposed by unified multi-signal observability and test the Bounded Resource Overhead Hypothesis ($H_{1,3}$), resource consumption was systematically profiled across Scenario~1 (Vanilla baseline) and Scenario~2 (PANOPTES-instrumented cluster). In both scenarios, the scoped OpenTelemetry Astronomy Shop (\textbf{ADR-0005}) operated under identical synthetic user traffic generated by the native Locust \textit{loadgenerator} without active fault injection across $N=30$ independent observations spaced by $16\,\text{s}$ ($480\,\text{s}$ duration, \textbf{ADR-0003}).

Table~\ref{tab:scenarios_1_2_comparison} summarizes the parametric (Mean $\pm$ Std) and non-parametric (Median and Interquartile Range --- IQR) distributions across both scenarios, isolating the monitored application workload ($R_{\text{workload}}$) from the dedicated PANOPTES observability infrastructure ($R_{\text{PANOPTES}}$).
